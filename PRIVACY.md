# Privacy Policy for Prof Lookup

Last updated: August 5, 2026

## What we collect

Nothing. Prof Lookup does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information about its users.

## What the extension does

When you view a professor's page on Rate My Professors, Prof Lookup reads
the professor's name and school identifier from that page in order to
search public Reddit posts about that professor. The results are displayed
in a sidebar on the same page.

These requests go directly from your browser to Reddit. We do not operate
any server, and no information about you or your browsing is sent to us or
to any third party.

## Local settings

Your theme preference (light, dark, or auto) and whether the sidebar is
collapsed are saved on your own device using Chrome's storage. These
settings never leave your computer.
